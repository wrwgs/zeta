Research Focus

My research focus is in the realm of functional programming & algebraic specification.
Language Design

The amalgamation of functional programming and algebraic specification concepts into a single language framework leads to an algebraic programming language.

I'am involved in the design of the algebraic programming language OPAL, being developed at the TU Berlin.

Language Translation & Optimization

To make algebraic programming feasible in practice, it is important that programs written in this style are comparable in space and time efficiency to programs written in low-level imperative languages such as C.

As the author of the OPAL compilers optimizing backend I try to contribute to this topic. My current research and PhD focus on exploiting algebraic-axiomatic properties for enhanced source-level optimizations.
