Current Work

I'm currently working in the ESPRESS project. ESPRESS is concerned with methods, techniques and tools for the development of embedded systems.

My research in ESPRESS focuses on the languages DZ and mSZ and their tool support and methodology. DZ is an extension of Z by temporal interval logics. mSZ is an extension of DZ by process classes, incorporating frontends with graphical notations such as Statecharts for behavioral descriptions.

The issues dealt with in my initial PhD project -- property-aided automatic program transformation of pure functional programs -- are currently on hold. My final PhD project is scheduled to be completed towards the end of 1998. I am investigating DZ and mSZ, and plan to compile a subset of these formalisms for synthesizing monitors for the behavior of reactive systems. This includes the compilation of a subset of Z.
